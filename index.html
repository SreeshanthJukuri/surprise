A Story I Didn't Expect ❤️

It started with a stupid line,

“Aren’t you mad at Neil Armstrong?”

You asked me why, I said, “Cuz he stepped on you,”

You called it corny, but somehow, our bond still grew.

A silly line on Hinge, that's all it seemed to be,

But somehow, talking to you felt so good to me.

One message became another, then hours would disappear,

And before I even knew it, I just loved having you here.

You gave me that fake ID, which you made to be in disguise,

And slowly, our conversations started growing in size.

Then one day you gave me your main, something you rarely do,

You said you never give it out, yet somehow, I got it from you.

I still remember how special that felt to me,

Because you don't just let everyone in so easily.

And somewhere between those messages and getting to know you,

I started realizing there was something different about you.

But we had our dry phase too,

When things didn't feel the same between me and you.

You'd send me a message, I'd just like it and leave,

Looking back at it now, I don't know what I was thinking.

For a while, our conversations lost their spark,

And I guess we both felt that change in the dark.

But somehow we found our way back, and I'm glad that we did,

Because losing what we had was never something I wanted, not even a bit.

Then comes our calls, where time forgets to move,

Where somehow every conversation felt so smooth.

I don't know what happens whenever I hear your voice,

But suddenly I'm a kid again, laughing without a choice.

I make the dumbest jokes, I giggle like a fool,

I forget how to act normal, and honestly, that's cool.

Because with you I don't have to pretend to be grown,

You bring out a version of me I didn't even know.

And then there's that dancing video you found on my profile,

The one that made you match with me and smile.

You thought it was cute, and that still makes me grin,

Then you showed your friends, and they called me cute.

It's funny how something as small as a dance,

Could somehow give two strangers a chance.

And now that I look at where we are today,

I'm really glad that video made you have my way.

And tonight, I know your heart isn't feeling okay,

You cried thinking about how much has changed along the way.

You looked back at your last birthday and everything that's new,

And maybe right now, all those changes feel heavy on you.

But Angelina, please remember this tonight,

Not every change that comes is meant to steal your light.

Some chapters end, some people leave, some things rearrange,

But sometimes life changes just to bring something strange.

Something you never planned,

Something you never knew you'd need,

Someone who walks into your life

and makes your heart breathe.

I can't promise that every birthday from now will be perfect,

Or that I'll always know exactly what to say when you're hurting.

But I can promise that whenever life feels unclear,

I'll be someone who listens, someone who stays near.

And if tonight feels different from the birthday you knew,

I hope this brings something beautiful to you.

Because you deserve laughter that doesn't have to hide,

And happiness that stays with you, not just for a night.

So happy birthday, Angelina,

My favourite person I met by fluke,

The girl who made a corny pickup line

turn into something I actually want to last.

And before this poem comes to an end,

There's one question I need to send.

We've laughed on calls, we've shared our days,

You've seen my childish, silly ways.

You gave me your main, you let me get close,

And somehow, you're the person I adore the most.

So maybe I'm being a bit brave,

Maybe I'm moving a little fast,

But I'd rather say what I feel

than wonder what could've happened in the past.

❤️

I Love You, Angelina.

Will you take my hand and let me call you my girl, and let me make you feel loved in this world where we met so unexpectedly?

Will you be my girlfriend?
You just made me the happiest person alive. ❤️

Happy Birthday, my baby. ♡
Made with way too much love for Angelina ❤️
— from the guy who started it all with the world's corniest pickup line.
